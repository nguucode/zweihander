Add the Stacked List pattern (`npx shadcn add .../r/stacked-list.json`): records as rows, each with an avatar or icon, a title and a line under it, a status, facts at the end that wrap under the text when narrow, and a link to the record (the whole row, with a chevron) or a ⋯ menu of actions that also opens on right click on an unlinked row. A `footer` takes a Pagination or a "View all" link, and `empty` replaces the list when there are no items.
