The last deliberate deviations from Atlassian are closed. ButtonGroup is a row of separate buttons 4px apart (it was a joined bar; ToggleGroup stays joined). Table's first and last cells are flush with its edges and the header has no fill; checkbox and actions columns are 24px. Modal sits 60px from the screen's sides and top (it was centred, 16px in) and fills the screen under 480px. Calendar days are 40×32 with no gap, its month buttons 24px; a date range picker shows one month under 50rem. Picker chevrons and clear icons are 12px, with 16px between a value and its chevron; DatePicker's calendar icon is 20px from the end. Field labels, a radio group's legend and a slider's label are weight 653 through the new `--font-weight-label` token (a static face rounds it, usually to 700). NavSidebar's head pads 12/4, its body 12, and its footer is ruled and padded 12; a label with no icon starts 10px in. The brand row and `header` slot of NavSidebar now sit in one wrapper element. Fixes: the first tab no longer sticks out of its list (its label starts at the list's edge), so it is not cut in a container that clips; CommandPalette's list is 12px from its edge; a Tag no longer inflates a line set in another font; ColorPicker's popup opens 8px under the whole field. Deprecated `size` props on Table and Pagination warn only for a non-default value. New token `--aside-width` (256px) for AppContent's aside and the Settings nav.
