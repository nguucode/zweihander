Add the Filter Bar pattern (`npx shadcn add …/r/filter-bar.json`): a search and per-field filter controls above a list or table, the applied filters as removable chips, and Clear all. Removing a chip keeps focus in the bar. Tag: a long text now truncates with an ellipsis when the tag is capped by `max-inline-size`, instead of overflowing.
