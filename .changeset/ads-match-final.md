The five places that still differed from Atlassian now match. Tabs: the underline list is pulled out by a tab's 8px padding again and the first tab keeps its padding, as Atlassian's; inside a container that clips, leave 8px of room on that side or the first tab's focus ring is cut. Calendar: the header is previous year, previous month, the title centred, next month, next year; the title reads the long month ("September 2026"); a year step that would pass `min` or `max` lands on the limit's month. Table: a table with rows closes on a 2px rule. NavSidebar: the head pads 12/12/4 with or without a logo and the brand row is 32px, so in the sidebar layout the logo sits 4px lower than before (its centre at 28px; 24px in the top-bar layout). ButtonGroup: one row that does not wrap.
