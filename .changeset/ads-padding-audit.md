Padding and spacing checked against the published @atlaskit packages and corrected. Fields: Select, Combobox, DatePicker and DateRangePicker put their value 9px from the edge and their chevron or calendar icon's centre 19px from the end (TextInput stays at 7px, as Atlassian's Textfield); option lists open 8px below the field, pad 8px above and below, and their rows are 32px (6×12); Textarea pads 6px (`md`) or 2px (`sm`) above and below with text 8px from the edge; the date panel pads 16px. Button and Tag no longer add their 1px border to the padding: a button's label is 12px from its edge, a tag's content 4px. Stacked radios are 4px apart (a 24px pitch; 8px on touch screens and when options have helper text); AvatarGroup overlaps 4px at every size, none at `sm`. Modal: 16px between title, body and footer. Toast and Alert: 16px between icon and text and 8px between title and description; Alert's actions sit 8px below, Toast's action is a 24px row directly under the description. EmptyState: 24px image to heading, 16px heading to description, 4px between buttons, 464px at most. Table caption: 24px above, 8px below. Tabs (underline): no gap between tabs, the indicator is inset 8px, and a horizontal list starts 8px out so labels line up with the content, which needs 8px of room in a container that clips. Pills keep their 4px gap. Menu: 12px inset, rows 2px apart, 12px between an item's parts, group labels 8/12. Pagination: no gap between page buttons. Breadcrumbs: 8px either side of a separator. TopBar: 4px between items within a group. NavSidebar and VerticalNav: rows touch, section headings are 32px rows, 12px between blocks. DataTablePage: 24px between the table and its pagination.
