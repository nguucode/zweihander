Add the Container pattern (registry only): caps a page's content at `sm` 48rem, `md` 64rem (default) or `lg` 80rem and centres it; `padded` adds side padding by breakpoint for a page outside the App Shell, where App Content does not pad it. App Content is unchanged. Install with `npx shadcn@latest add https://ontheshore.biz/zweihander/r/container.json`.
