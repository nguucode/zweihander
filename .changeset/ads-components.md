Components follow Atlassian's box sizing. Button: 12px inline padding, a 6px icon gap and a 14/20 label at both sizes (`sm` was 8px and 12/16). Tabs: 8px inline padding. Menu, ContextMenu, Menubar and Select options: 36px rows (8×12 padding), popups 8px radius. Checkbox and Radio: a 16px box (was 20) in a 24px hit area. Switch: `md` 32×16 and `lg` 40×20; `size="sm"` is deprecated and renders `md`. Tag: one 20px size with 12/16 text, capped at 180px; its `size` prop is deprecated. Badge: counts are 16px tall, at least 24px wide, 2px radius; `size="sm"` on a count is deprecated. Modal: 400 / 600 / 800px wide, 24px padding, a 20/24 title. Drawer: 360 / 480 / 600px. Popover, Card, Toast and Alert: 8px radius; Toast is 400px wide with 16px padding, Alert has 16px padding. Tooltip: 4×6 padding, 240px at most. Table: cells are 4×8 (`md`) or 2×8 (`sm`) and a row is as tall as its content (28 / 24px for one line of text, 40px with a 32px control); the header is 12/16 weight 600 over a 2px rule; the `density` prop is renamed `size`, and `density` is deprecated. TopBar: 48px tall (was 56) with 12px inline padding and 8px gaps; NavSidebar pads 12px and its rail is 56px. Token values: `--control-md-gap` is 6px (was 8) and `--control-sm-pad-spacious` 12px (was 8). The component-private custom properties `--tag-icon` and `--table-row` are gone. Remove the deprecated `size` values and rename `density` to `size` on Table.
