Foundations

One layer of tokens. Everything else reads from it.

Colour, type, spacing, radius and elevation are named by what they do, not how they look, so a re-brand is a change of values, not a hunt through components.

17 accents, 4 grays. Red to rose, slate to neutral, every one a 12-step ramp.

Light and dark. 129 semantic colours with a value for each appearance.

Contrast is measured. Each accent takes the first step that clears 4.5:1 for its label.

16 text styles. Six headings and three body sizes in three weights.

A 4px grid. Spacing, control heights and icons all sit on it.

Three corner presets. Sharp, rounded and full rounded, applied by role.

Components

The parts every product screen is made of.

Buttons to date pickers to charts, each with its variants, sizes and states. Focus, ARIA and keyboard handling come built in.

Questions? Here are answers.

The rest is in the docs.

Who is Zweihänder for?

Product teams who design in Figma and build in React, and want one source for both. It leans toward dense product UI: CRMs, admin tools, finance and accounting screens.

Where do I get the Figma file?

On Figma Community: Zweihänder Design System. Duplicate it into your team and use it as a library.

How do Figma and code stay in sync?

Code is the source of the tokens. A change is made there, checked for contrast, then exported to the Figma variables, so the two never drift apart.

Can we make it look like our brand?

Yes. Pick an accent, a gray and a corner preset, then override any token. Light and dark come from the same scale.

Is it accessible?

Focus, ARIA and keyboard handling come from Base UI, accent labels are measured against 4.5:1, and every component documents its keyboard map.

Is it ready for production?

Not yet. It is 0.x, and a minor version can still break. Components marked experimental, like the charts, change fastest.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It is MIT licensed.

Bring it into your product

Copy the source with the shadcn CLI, or install the package from npm.

npm install zweihander
