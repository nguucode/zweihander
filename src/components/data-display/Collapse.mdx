# Collapse

<DocTabs>
<DocTab value="overview">

One show/hide section, such as "Show details" or "Advanced settings": a
text button with a chevron, and the content it reveals under it.

<Status of={CollapseStories} />

<Canvas of={CollapseStories.Open} />

## Variants

Collapse has one look. `size` matches it to the text around it, and
`openLabel` decides whether the label changes when it opens.

| Size | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| `md` | Body text, 14px. The default. |
| `sm` | Small text, 12px: captions, dense panels, notes under a field. |

<Canvas of={CollapseStories.Default} />

<Canvas of={CollapseStories.Small} />

| Label | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| With `openLabel` | A label that names the action: "Show details" becomes "Hide details". |
| Without `openLabel` | A label that names the section: "Advanced settings" stays the same, and the chevron shows the state. |

- **Hover** underlines the label, like a link.
- **Focus** is a 2px `--ring` outline, 2px outside the trigger.
- **Pressed** has no look of its own: the label stays underlined while the
  pointer is down.
- **Open** turns the chevron from right to down, shows `openLabel` if
  there is one, and animates the panel's height over `--duration-base`.
- **Disabled** dims the trigger to 50% opacity, with no underline on hover.

## When to use

Use a Collapse for one aside in running content: billing details under a
plan, advanced settings under a form. It is a text button, not a bar,
because it belongs to the content around it, and its trigger reads like a
link in a sentence.

<DoDont
  do={<Story of={CollapseStories.DoAsideInText} />}
  doCaption="One aside, under the text it belongs to. The label says what opening it does."
  dont={<Story of={CollapseStories.DontStackAsList} />}
  dontCaption="A stack of Collapses is a list of sections. Use an Accordion: its items are real headings, framed and divided."
/>

## When not to use

- **For a list of sections.** Use an
  [Accordion](?path=/docs/components-data-display-accordion--docs). Its
  items are full-width bars with dividers because they are the page's
  structure.
- **For a short hint about a control.** Use a
  [Tooltip](?path=/docs/components-overlays-tooltip--docs) or a
  [Popover](?path=/docs/components-overlays-popover--docs), which do not
  push the page down.
- **For content most readers need.** Hiding it costs everyone a click.
  Show it.

## Accessibility

- The trigger is a `<button>` with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`
  pointing at the panel.
- Enter or Space toggles it. With `openLabel`, the button's name changes
  with its state ("Show details" / "Hide details"), and `aria-expanded`
  says it either way. Without it, the name stays the same.
- The chevron is decorative (`aria-hidden`), so it adds nothing to the
  name.
- Closed content is `hidden="until-found"` by default, so find-in-page
  reaches it.
- The trigger grows to a 44px target on coarse pointers.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the panel and chevron change without
  animating.
