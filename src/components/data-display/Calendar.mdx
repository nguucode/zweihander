# Calendar

A month grid to pick one day from, or to show one: a booking page, the
inside of a Date Picker. Spec:
[uiguideline.com/components/calendar](https://www.uiguideline.com/components/calendar).
A plain element, using `Intl` for names and the week's first day: no
date library.

<Canvas of={CalendarStories.Default} />

## Anatomy

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Header** | The short month and year ("Oct 2026") at the start, previous and next month buttons together at the end. |
| **Weekdays** | Two letters ("Mo"), or the narrow names ("T2") where the short ones are long; the locale's first day first. |
| **Day** | A 32px button (44px on touch), 4px apart. Days of the months either side are decoration: faded (`--muted-foreground` at 50%), hidden from assistive tech, not pickable, never selected or in a range. Arrow keys still cross into them by moving the month. |
| **Today** | An `--orange` disc with an `--orange-foreground` (white) figure, round where a selected day is square. White on orange is 3.4:1, under WCAG's 4.5:1: a deliberate choice; `aria-current="date"` carries it for assistive tech. |
| **Selected** | `--primary` (the brand) behind `--primary-foreground`. Wins over today when they are the same day. |
| **Range** | With `range`: both ends selected, the days between on an `--accent` band as tall as a day, rounded where a week or the month starts or ends. Days of the months either side are not banded, so two months side by side never draw the same day twice. |
| **Unavailable** | Muted and struck through; before `min`, after `max`, or ruled out by `isDateDisabled`. |

## Props

| Prop | Values | Default | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `value`, `defaultValue`, `onValueChange` | `Date` | `null` | Local midnight; times are ignored. |
| `month`, `defaultMonth`, `onMonthChange` | `Date` | value's or today's | The month shown. |
| `min`, `max` | `Date` | – | |
| `isDateDisabled` | `(date) => boolean` | – | Rule out single days. |
| `range` | `{ start, end }` | – | Shows a span instead of `value`. Clicks still report one day; the owner decides the span, as Date Range Picker does. |
| `locale` | BCP 47 tag | the browser's | Month and weekday names. |
| `weekStartsOn` | 0–6 | the locale's | 0 is Sunday. |
| `autoFocus` | boolean | `false` | Focus the selected day, e.g. inside a popover. |

`isSameDay`, `addDays`, `addMonths`, `startOfDay` and `firstDayOfWeek`
are exported for working with the same plain dates.

### Design notes

- **Six rows always.** Months span four to six weeks; a fixed height
  keeps a popover or a page from jumping as you page through them.
- **The locale decides the week.** `vi-VN` and `en-GB` start on Monday,
  `en-US` on Sunday, read from `Intl.Locale` where the browser knows it
  (Monday otherwise).
- **Month steps keep the day where they can.** 31 January plus a month
  is 28 February, not 3 March.

## Accessibility

- A `role="grid"` named by the month and year, with column headers
  carrying the full weekday name. The selected cell has
  `aria-selected`, today's day `aria-current="date"`, and each day
  button the full date as its name ("Friday 18 September 2026").
- One tab stop: the selected day (or today). Arrows move a day or a
  week, Home and End go to the week's ends, Page Up and Page Down move a
  month and Shift+Page Up/Down a year, Enter or Space selects. Moving
  past the month shows the next one.

  <Canvas of={CalendarStories.Keyboard} />

- The month and year are a polite live region, so paging is announced.
- Unavailable days stay focusable with `aria-disabled`, so they are
  found and announced as unavailable rather than skipped silently.
