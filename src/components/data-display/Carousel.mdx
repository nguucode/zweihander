# Carousel

A row of slides seen one (or a few) at a time: featured items, a gallery,
onboarding cards.

## Variants

The carousel has no `variant` prop. These are its configurations:

| Configuration | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| One in view (default) | Large slides that each need the full width: a feature, a photo. |
| `slidesPerView={3}` | Smaller items browsed in a row: templates, products. |
| `loop` | A short set with no natural first or last, where next from the end should wrap. |
| `autoPlay` | Rarely. A showcase where motion is the point; it adds a stop/start button. |

<Canvas of={CarouselStories.ThreeInView} />

With three in view, five slides have three stops (1-3, 2-4, 3-5), and the
dots count stops, not slides.

## Anatomy

<Anatomy parts={CarouselStories.anatomyParts}>
  <Story of={CarouselStories.Anatomy} />
</Anatomy>

The controls sit above the track, at its end, so they never cover a slide.
A visually hidden line after the track says where the reader is (see
Accessibility).

- **Native scrolling.** No transform maths and no gesture library: the
  track is `overflow-x: auto` with `scroll-snap-type`, so touch, trackpad
  and momentum behave as the platform does. The index is read back once
  the track comes to rest.
- **Right to left works.** Offsets are measured from the first slide, so
  the negative `scrollLeft` of a right-to-left track reads correctly, and
  the previous and next chevrons are mirrored.
- **`defaultIndex` starts there.** The track jumps to it on mount, without
  a scroll animation.

- **Hover** fills an enabled button with `--accent` and its icon with
  `--accent-foreground`. Dots have no hover look.
- **Focus** is a 2px `--ring` outline, 2px outside the track, a button or a
  dot.
- **Pressed** has no look of its own.
- **Disabled**: previous at the first stop and next at the last, unless
  `loop`. A disabled button is the enabled look at 50% opacity, with the
  disabled cursor and no hover.
- **Current slide**: the current stop's dot is the longer `--foreground`
  pill.

## When to use

Everything in a carousel past the first slide is hidden until the reader
goes looking. Use one for content that is fine to miss: featured items,
examples, a gallery. Give it a `label` that names what is in it.

<DoDont
  do={<Story of={CarouselStories.Default} />}
  doCaption="Featured templates: browsing is optional, and missing one costs nothing."
  dont={<Story of={CarouselStories.DontHideImportant} />}
  dontCaption="Account notices the reader must act on, four of them hidden in slides most readers never reach."
/>

Prefer no `autoPlay`. Motion pulls the eye away from the rest of the page,
and a slide can move on before it has been read.

## When not to use

- **For content everyone must see.** Show it at once: a list, or
  [Cards](?path=/docs/components-data-display-card--docs) in a grid, show
  more and hide nothing.
- **For items to compare.** Use a
  [Table](?path=/docs/components-data-display-table--docs): side by side
  beats one at a time.
- **For steps in a flow.** Use a
  [Stepper](?path=/docs/components-navigation-stepper--docs): it shows how
  many steps there are and where you are.

## Accessibility

Follows the [WAI-ARIA carousel pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/carousel/).

- A `<section>` with `aria-roledescription="carousel"` and your `label`;
  each slide is a group with `aria-roledescription="slide"` and a name
  like "2 of 5".
- Previous, next, stop/start and the dots are native buttons: Tab reaches
  them, Enter and Space press them.
- Previous and next are disabled at the ends unless `loop`. Dots are
  buttons named "Slide 3", with `aria-current` on the current stop.
- **Autoplay** (WCAG 2.2.2) pauses while the pointer is over the carousel
  or focus is inside it, has a stop/start button as its first control, and
  never runs under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- A visually hidden line says where you are ("Slide 3 of 5", or "Slides
  3 to 5 of 5"). It is `aria-live="polite"` when the reader moves the
  carousel, so a screen reader hears the change, and `off` while it
  rotates on its own. Every slide is in the DOM from the start, so a live
  region round the track would stay silent.
- The track is in the tab order (`tabindex="0"`), like any scrolling
  region.
- In right to left, the previous and next chevrons are mirrored.
- Buttons and dots have 44px touch targets on coarse pointers.
