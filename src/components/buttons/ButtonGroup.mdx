# Button Group

<DocTabs>
<DocTab value="overview">

Buttons joined into one bar. `ToggleGroup` joins
[Toggle Buttons](?path=/docs/components-buttons-togglebutton--docs) that
share one selection; `ButtonGroup` joins plain
[Buttons](?path=/docs/components-buttons-button--docs) that act.

<Status of={ButtonGroupStories} />

Button Group is outside the 1.0 component list, so it starts as experimental.

<Canvas of={ButtonGroupStories.Default} />

## Variants

| Form | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| `ToggleGroup`, single | A segmented control: one view, one mode, one period at a time. |
| `ToggleGroup multiple` | Settings that combine: status filters, Bold and Italic. |
| `ButtonGroup` | Related actions that read as a set: previous and next, zoom in and out. |

## Measurements

The group adds no size of its own. Each button keeps its tier, so a group
of `sm` buttons is `--control-sm` tall. Inner corners are square and each
button after the first overlaps the one before by its 1px border, so two
outlined buttons share one line. The outer corners keep the buttons'
radius.

## Color

The group paints nothing: unpressed and pressed colours are the Toggle
Button's, and a ButtonGroup's are each Button's. The pressed toggle is
raised over its neighbours so its own border wins where they overlap.

## When to use

Use `ToggleGroup` when the toggles answer one question (which view, which
filters) and `ButtonGroup` when a few actions belong together. Give every
button in a group the same variant and appearance, so the pressed one is
the only one that stands out.

## When not to use

- **To switch between panels of content.** Use Tabs: they own the panel
  and its `tabpanel` role.
- **For a choice submitted with a form.** Use a Radio group or Checkboxes:
  a toggle applies at once, a form field waits for Submit.
- **For unrelated actions.** Lay out separate Buttons with a gap; joining
  them says they belong together.
- **For more than a handful of options.** Use a Select.

## Accessibility

- Both containers have `role="group"`; name them with `aria-label`.
- Each toggle is a native `<button>` with `aria-pressed`, so a screen reader
  announces "Board, toggle button, pressed".
- A `ToggleGroup` is one tab stop. Arrow keys move between its toggles
  and wrap, Home and End jump to the first and last; Space or Enter
  presses.
- Single choice keeps one pressed: pressing the pressed toggle again does
  not release it.
- A `ButtonGroup`'s buttons are each a tab stop, with no `aria-pressed`.
- A disabled `ToggleGroup` disables every toggle in it.
- An icon-only button needs its own `aria-label`, as on Button. A
  [Tooltip](?path=/docs/components-overlays-tooltip--docs) can show that
  label to sighted users: it merges onto the button instead of wrapping
  it, so the buttons still join.
