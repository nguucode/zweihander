# Success State

<DocTabs>
<DocTab value="overview">

What a flow shows when it has finished: the invites are sent, the import
is done, the inbox is empty. It confirms what happened and offers the
next step.

<Status of={SuccessStateStories} />

<Canvas of={SuccessStateStories.Default} />

## Variants

| Variant | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| `md` | The end of a flow that filled the page or a dialog: a sent form, a finished import. Lead with the next step. |
| `sm` | A panel or a list that is done: "All caught up". Often no action. |

<Canvas of={SuccessStateStories.Small} />

## When to use

Use a Success State when the screen the reader was on is finished and
needs replacing: a sent form, a completed import, an emptied inbox. Say
what was done, and lead with the next step.

<DoDont
  do={<Story of={SuccessStateStories.Default} />}
  doCaption="The flow is over: say what was done and where to go next."
  dont={<Story of={SuccessStateStories.DontForQuickSave} />}
  dontCaption="A quick save is not the end of a flow. This hides the form; a Toast would leave it in place."
/>

## When not to use

- **To confirm a quick action** such as Save, Copy or Move. Use a Toast:
  it confirms and leaves the screen as it was.
- **When there is nothing there yet.** Use an
  [Empty State](?path=/docs/components-states-emptystate--docs).

## Accessibility

- The title is a real heading, as on Empty State. Set `headingLevel` to
  fit the outline.
- The icon is `aria-hidden`; the title says what was done.
- `isLive` makes it a `role="status"`, read politely when it replaces a
  form or a progress view. Without it, nothing is announced.
