# Date Range Picker

<DocTabs>
<DocTab value="overview">

A field for a span of days: a report period, a booking. Typed like
[Date Picker](?path=/docs/components-inputs-datepicker--docs), or picked
from two months side by side.

<Status of={DateRangePickerStories} />

<Canvas of={DateRangePickerStories.Default} />

## Variants

Two sizes, `sm` and `md`, and the field's validation states, as on Date
Picker. With `presets`, whole periods sit beside the calendars.

| Option | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| No presets | Ranges that rarely line up with a calendar period: a booking, a leave request. |
| `presets` | Reports and filters, where people mostly want "Last month" or "This year". |

The popover holds:

- **Presets**, with `presets`: whole periods, Today to Last year, in a
  column beside the calendars, or a row above them on a narrow screen.
- **Calendars**: two months that page on their own, opening on the
  range's first and last months (this month and the next when empty). The
  left one always stays before the right: paging one past the other
  pushes the other along. One month on a screen narrower than 40rem.

### Design notes

- **Either way round.** The first click starts the range, the second
  ends it; a second click before the first swaps them. So does typing
  the later date first.
- **Presets are whole periods.** "This month" is the 1st to the last
  day, not the 1st to today, so a preset reads the same all month.
- **No Cancel or Apply.** A range applies, and the popover closes, once
  both ends are in; Escape before the second click leaves the value as
  it was.
- **No hover preview.** The band shows once both ends are in; add a
  preview if testing shows people lose track of the first click.

## When to use

Use a Date Range Picker when the two dates are one thing: a period to
report on, a stay, a sprint. Both ends are set together, start never after
end, and the value is complete or `null`.

<DoDont
  do={<Story of={DateRangePickerStories.DoOneField} />}
  doCaption="One field for one period: it cannot be half filled or set back to front."
  dont={<Story of={DateRangePickerStories.DontTwoFields} />}
  dontCaption="Two Date Pickers for one period: here the end is before the start, and nothing stops it."
/>

## When not to use

- **For one date.** Use a
  [Date Picker](?path=/docs/components-inputs-datepicker--docs).
- **When one end is optional**, such as a contract with no end date. The
  range needs both ends; use two Date Pickers and check their order
  yourself.

## Accessibility

- As Date Picker: a labelled text box, an error state with the expected
  pattern, and the calendars in a dialog named "Choose dates".
- The calendar button is named "Choose dates", with the current range
  when there is one.
- Every day in the range has `aria-selected`, so the span is announced,
  not only its ends.
- A day of the month either side is decoration: hidden from assistive
  tech and not pickable, so each day is one button.
- Presets are toggle buttons in a group named "Presets"; the one that
  matches the picked range is `aria-pressed`.
- After both ends are in the field goes back to rest, as Date Picker's
  does after a pick.
