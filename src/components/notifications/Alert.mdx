# Alert

<DocTabs>
<DocTab value="overview">

A message about a page or a section that stays until the situation changes
or the reader dismisses it: something went wrong, needs attention, or worked.

<Status of={AlertStories} />

<Canvas of={AlertStories.WithAction} />

## Variants

`variant` is the colour, by meaning; `appearance` is the emphasis. Any
variant takes any appearance.

<Canvas of={AlertStories.Variants} />

| Variant | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| `info` | Something worth knowing that changes nothing: a plan change, scheduled maintenance. |
| `success` | Something finished and stays true: a domain verified, an import done. |
| `warning` | Something that will go wrong if nobody acts: a card expiring, a quota nearly used. |
| `danger` | Something that went wrong and needs a next step: a failed upload, a lost connection. |

<Canvas of={AlertStories.Appearances} />

| Appearance | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| `subtle` | The default. Sits on the page without shouting. |
| `outlined` | On a tinted or busy background, where a tint would disappear. |
| `solid` | The one message nobody should miss, such as an outage banner. Rarely more than one per page. |

The border is always there, 1px and transparent unless `outlined`, so
switching appearance never moves the content. The padding counts that
pixel: the content sits 16px from the alert's edge. The close button is 24px,
flush with the padding and top-aligned with the icon. No shadow: an alert is
part of the page.

the colour goes on the icon, the title and the close button.

## States

The alert itself has no states. Its close button does.

<States
  states={[
    { label: 'Enabled' },
    { label: 'Close hover', pseudo: 'hover' },
    { label: 'Close focus', pseudo: 'focus-visible' },
  ]}
>
  <Story of={AlertStories.Dismissible} />
</States>

- **Hover** tints the close button with 15% of the alert's own text
  colour, so it reads on every variant and appearance.
- **Focus** is a 2px `--color-border-focused` outline, inside the button's edge.

### Differences from the spec

- **Sized after Atlassian's section message**: 16px padding, 16px from
  the icon to the text, 8px between title, description and actions, 8px
  corners, no shadow, a semibold 14/20 title, a 24px close button.

- **Placed in the content, not at the top of the screen.** The spec
  describes a prominent message at the top; here an alert goes where the
  thing it is about is. An outage banner is a `solid` alert placed at the
  top by the page.
- **Closing is the parent's job.** `onClose` reports the click and the
  alert stays until whoever rendered it removes it, so "dismissed" lives in
  your state and can be remembered across visits.

## When to use

Use an Alert for a message about a page or a section that should stay put
until it is dealt with: a failed save, a card about to expire, an import
that finished. The title says what happened; the description says what it
means and what to do.

<DoDont
  do={<Story of={AlertStories.DoSayWhatHappened} />}
  doCaption="Say what happened and offer the next step."
  dont={<Story of={AlertStories.DontVague} />}
  dontCaption="“Something went wrong” leaves the red box to do the talking, and the reader with nothing to do."
/>

The icon is decorative, so the variant has to be clear from the words too:
"Upload failed", not just a red box.

## When not to use

- **For news about something that just happened and needs nothing more.**
  Use a [Toast](?path=/docs/components-notifications-toast--docs): it goes
  away by itself.
- **For a one-line message under a form or a group of fields.** Use an
  [Inline Alert](?path=/docs/components-notifications-inlinealert--docs).
- **For an error on one field.** Use the field's own `validationState` and
  `helperText`, tied to it with `aria-describedby`.
- **For a decision the reader must make before going on.** Use a Modal.

## Accessibility

- A plain `<div>` by default: an alert on the page when it loads is read in
  order, like the text around it, and does not interrupt.
- An alert that **appears** in response to something (a failed save) needs
  `isLive`. `danger` and `warning` become `role="alert"` and are read at
  once; `info` and `success` become `role="status"` and wait for the screen
  reader to finish.

  <Canvas of={AlertStories.Live} />

- The icon is `aria-hidden`: the words carry the meaning.
- The close button is a native `<button>` named `Dismiss` (change it with
  `closeLabel`). Tab reaches it, Enter and Space press it.
- Focus ring: 2px `--color-border-focused` on the close button. On touch screens its hit
  area grows to 44×44px without the button growing.
- Every variant and appearance passes the story tests' contrast check
  (light mode).
