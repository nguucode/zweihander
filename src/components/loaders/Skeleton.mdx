# Skeleton

<DocTabs>
<DocTab value="overview">

A placeholder in the shape of content that is still loading, so the page
keeps its layout and the reader sees what is coming.

<Status of={SkeletonStories} />

<Canvas of={SkeletonStories.CardPlaceholder} />

## Variants

`appearance` sets the corners, to match what the shape stands in for. Any
appearance takes any size.

<Canvas of={SkeletonStories.Appearances} />

| Appearance | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| `circle` | An avatar or a round icon. Its height is its width. |
| `square` | The default: an image, a media block, anything with square corners. |
| `rounded` | Text, buttons and cards: anything with the kit's rounded corners. |

`rows` stacks lines of text, the last one shorter so it reads as a
paragraph rather than a block.

<Canvas of={SkeletonStories.Rows} />

layout of the content that will replace them.

## Measurements

A skeleton has no sizes: `width` and `height` copy the content it stands
in for. These are the defaults, and the parts it sizes itself.

  caption="Every appearance"

The default width is the container's. The last of several `rows` is 60%
of the width.

## Color

Every shape is one colour, the same subtle surface as a hover. It is below
any contrast floor on purpose: a placeholder should read as "something
goes here", not compete with content that has arrived. Switch the theme in
the toolbar: the value follows.

## States

A skeleton is not interactive: it has no hover, focus or pressed state.

<States
  states={[
    { label: 'Static' },
    { label: 'Pulsing', content: <div style={card}><Skeleton appearance="rounded" rows={3} height={12} width={200} hasAnimation /></div> },
  ]}
>
  <div style={card}>
    <Skeleton appearance="rounded" rows={3} height={12} width={200} />
  </div>
</States>

- **Static** is the default.
- **Pulsing**, with `hasAnimation`, fades to 50% and back every 1.6s. It
  stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## When to use

Use a Skeleton when you know the shape of what is loading: a list, a card,
a profile. Match the shapes to the content that will replace them, with
the same sizes and the same gaps.

<DoDont
  do={<Story of={SkeletonStories.CardPlaceholder} />}
  doCaption="Shapes of the content that is coming: the layout holds when it arrives."
  dont={<Story of={SkeletonStories.DontOneBlock} />}
  dontCaption="One block for a whole card jumps to a different layout on load, which is worse than a spinner."
/>

Animate the few shapes that matter, or none: `hasAnimation` is off by
default because a screen of pulsing shapes is noise.

<Canvas of={SkeletonStories.Animated} />

## When not to use

- **When you do not know the shape of what is coming.** Use a
  [Spinner](?path=/docs/components-loaders-spinner--docs).
- **For a long task with a known size.** Use a
  [Progress Bar](?path=/docs/components-loaders-progressbar--docs).
- **For a load that is usually instant.** A skeleton that flashes for
  200ms is noise; show nothing, or a Spinner with a `delay`.

## Accessibility

- **A skeleton is decoration** and is `aria-hidden`, with its `rows`
  hidden as one. It says nothing to a screen reader.
- The region it stands in for has to say it is loading: put
  `aria-busy="true"` on it, as the CardPlaceholder story does, and remove
  it when the content arrives.
- If the wait matters, announce it: a visually hidden status, or a Spinner
  with a label.
- `--muted` is below 3:1 on the page in both modes, on purpose. It is not
  content, and should not compete with content that has arrived.
- The pulse stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
