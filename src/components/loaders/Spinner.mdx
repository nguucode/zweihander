# Spinner

<DocTabs>
<DocTab value="overview">

Says that something is loading when there is no telling how long it will
take, or, given a value, how far along it is.

<Status of={SpinnerStories} />

<Canvas of={SpinnerStories.WithLabel} />

## Variants

`variant` is the colour of the indicator. The track and the label are the
same in all three.

<Canvas of={SpinnerStories.Variants} />

| Variant | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| <Spinner variant="accent" /> `accent` | The default: a quiet placeholder in `--foreground`, inside content that has its own colour. |
| <Spinner variant="primary" /> `primary` | A load the page is about, where the brand colour helps it be found: the first load of a view. |
| <Spinner variant="secondary" /> `secondary` | Low emphasis: a spinner beside other muted text, or in a dense list. |

With a `value` it stops spinning and becomes a progress ring, which reads
better than a bar in a small space such as a thumbnail.

## States

A spinner is not interactive: it has no hover, focus or pressed state.

<States
  states={[
    { label: 'Spinning' },
    { label: 'Progress', content: <Spinner value={40} label="Uploading" variant="primary" /> },
    { label: 'Complete', content: <Spinner value={100} label="Uploaded" variant="primary" /> },
  ]}
>
  <Story of={SpinnerStories.WithLabel} />
</States>

- **Spinning** is the default, and what it shows with `isIndeterminate`
  even when given a `value`.
- **Progress** fills the ring clockwise from twelve o'clock, easing
  between values over `--duration-slow`.
- **Delayed**: with `delay`, nothing shows until it has passed. The live
  region is in the page, empty, from the start.

## When to use

Use a Spinner when something is loading and you cannot say what its result
will look like or how long it will take. Use one per region that is
loading, labelled with what is coming.

<DoDont
  do={<Story of={SpinnerStories.DoOneForTheRegion} />}
  doCaption="One spinner for the region, named for what is coming."
  dont={<Story of={SpinnerStories.DontOnePerItem} />}
  dontCaption="A spinner per card says the same thing three times, and three screen-reader announcements."
/>

Give a load you expect to be fast a `delay` of 300–500ms. The spinner only
appears if the wait turns out to be long enough to notice.

<Canvas of={SpinnerStories.Delay} />

## When not to use

- **When you know the shape of what is coming.** A
  [Skeleton](?path=/docs/components-loaders-skeleton--docs) keeps the
  layout and says more.
- **For a long task with a known size**: an upload, an install. Use a
  [Progress Bar](?path=/docs/components-loaders-progressbar--docs), or a
  `value` here when space is tight.
- **Inside a button.** Use the Button's `isLoading`: it keeps the button's
  width and focus and stops a second click.

## Accessibility

- **Spinning, it is `role="status"`**, a polite live region, named by
  `label` or "Loading". The region renders empty and its text (the label,
  or a visually hidden "Loading") is inserted just after, or once `delay`
  has passed: screen readers announce text added to a live region that is
  already in the page, not a region that mounts full.
- **With a value, it is `role="progressbar"`** with `aria-valuenow`,
  `aria-valuemin="0"` and `aria-valuemax="100"`. Values outside 0–100 are
  clamped.
- The SVG is hidden.
- The indicator clears 3:1 on the page in every variant: `--primary-text`
  (measured at 4.5:1 per accent), `--muted-foreground`, `--foreground`.
  The track is decoration and may be faint.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion` it slows to one turn in 2.5s rather than
  stopping, since a frozen arc reads as "stuck", and the progress ring
  stops easing between values.
- Put `aria-busy="true"` on the region that is loading, and remove the
  spinner when the load ends, so the change is announced.
