# Avatar

<DocTabs>
<DocTab value="overview">

A picture of a person or account, with initials or a silhouette when there
is no picture, an optional presence dot, and a group that overlaps several.

<Status of={AvatarStories} />

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.Fallbacks} />

## Variants

Neutral by default. `color` picks any accent hue, and `variant` how strong
it is. Behind a photo the colour only shows while it loads or where the
image is transparent.

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.Colors} />

| Variant | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| <Avatar initials="SR" /> `subtle` | Most places: lists, comments, a byline. A tint that sits quietly next to text. |
| <Avatar initials="SR" variant="solid" /> `solid` | Where the avatar has to stand out on its own: a header, an account menu trigger. |

Colour tells people apart in a list; it carries no meaning. Pick it from the
person (a hash of their id), never from a status.

### Sizes

Atlassian's six. `md` is the default.

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.Sizes} />

| `size` | Across | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `xs` | 16px | Inline with a line of small text: a mention, a dense table cell. One initial. |
| `sm` | 24px | Beside a name in a row, a field or a table cell. |
| `md` | 32px | The default: a comment, a feed event, the account button in a top bar. |
| `lg` | 40px | The leading visual of a list row or a card. |
| `xl` | 96px | A profile header. |
| `2xl` | 128px | A profile page, or the picture being edited. |

With initials, `xs` shows the first letter only: two do not fit in 16px.

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.InitialsSizes} />

### Status

`status` puts a presence dot on the bottom-end corner, in Badge's presence
colours: online, away, busy, offline.

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.Status} />

The dot grows with the avatar, and at `xl` and `2xl` it sits on the curve:

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.StatusSizes} />

### Group

`AvatarGroup` overlaps a row of avatars, for the people on a record or a
thread. `max` is the most circles in the row, the "+n" included.

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.Group} />

With `hasTooltips`, each named avatar shows its name on hover, and the
"+n" lists the people it stands for, on hover or keyboard focus:

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.GroupTooltips} />

The image fills the circle edge to edge. The silhouette is the kit's
`user` icon at 60% of the circle, centred.

Initials are 12px up to `md`, where `xs` keeps only the first letter, 14px
on `lg`, then 28px and 32px: the type scale stops at 32px, so on `xl` and
`2xl` the letters are a smaller share of the circle. They are medium (500)
up to `lg` and semibold (600) on `xl` and `2xl`, Figma's Heading/xl and
Heading/2xl.

An `xs` avatar draws no status dot, as in Atlassian: at 16px the dot would
cover most of the picture, so the status is in the accessible name only.
The status dot is an 8px Badge dot (`md`) on the sm, md and lg avatars,
flush on the corner of the avatar's box, outside the curve. On `xl` and `2xl` that corner is far off the circle and a Badge dot
is a speck, so the dot is 16px and 20px, and its centre sits on the circle
at 45°.

## States

An avatar is not interactive: no hover, focus or pressed. Its states are
which part it can show.

<States
  states={[
    { label: 'Image', content: <Avatar size="lg" imageSrc={AvatarStories.default.args.imageSrc} imageAlt="Mary Thompson" /> },
    { label: 'Initials', content: <Avatar size="lg" initials="SR" imageAlt="Sam Rivera" /> },
    { label: 'Silhouette', content: <Avatar size="lg" imageAlt="Unknown user" /> },
    { label: 'Broken image', content: <Story of={AvatarStories.BrokenImage} /> },
    { label: 'Online', content: <Avatar size="lg" initials="SR" imageAlt="Sam Rivera" status="online" /> },
    { label: 'Offline', content: <Avatar size="lg" initials="SR" imageAlt="Sam Rivera" status="offline" /> },
  ]}
/>

- **Image** shows while `imageSrc` loads and after.
- **Broken image** drops the `<img>` and shows the initials, or the
  silhouette when there are none, instead of the browser's broken-image
  icon. A new `imageSrc` is tried again. An empty `imageSrc` is no image.

### Differences from the spec

- **`initials` is added.** The spec's Anatomy has an Initials part but
  its prop list has no way to set them. Pass the letters, not a name:
  how to abbreviate a name depends on the language, and the kit should
  not guess.
- **A broken image falls back** to initials or the silhouette instead
  of showing the browser's broken-image icon.
- **Sizes follow Atlassian Design**: 16, 24, 32, 40, 96 and
  128px, with `md` (32px) the default, so an avatar lines up with the
  kit's 32px controls. There is no 48px.
- **`xs` shows one initial.** Two letters do not fit in 16px at the
  smallest type size.
- **No square, no `appearance`.** Always a circle, so a person never
  reads as a product or workspace logo.
- **The placeholder is the kit's `user` icon** at 60% of the circle,
  centred, as in Figma. It takes `color` and `variant` like initials do.
- **`color` and `variant` are added**, to tell people apart in a list.
- **The circle ignores the radius preset.**
- **`status` and `AvatarGroup` are added.** The status words and the
  "+n" name are English by default; `statusLabel` and `moreLabel` translate
  them.
- **`max` counts the "+n".** The row never grows past `max` circles, and
  the "+n" always stands for two or more: never "+1" where the person
  would have fitted.

## When to use

Use an Avatar to show who: the author of a comment, the people on a
project, the account that is signed in. Give it `imageAlt` when the avatar
is the only thing that says who it is. When the name is printed next to
it, leave `imageAlt` out: the avatar is decorative, and the name is read
once.

<DoDont
  do={<Story of={AvatarStories.DoDecorativeBesideName} />}
  doCaption="The name is printed, so the avatar has no imageAlt. A screen reader hears “Mary Thompson” once."
  dont={<Story of={AvatarStories.DontRepeatName} />}
  dontCaption="imageAlt repeats the printed name: “Mary Thompson, image, Mary Thompson”."
/>

## When not to use

- **For a product, team or workspace logo.** An Avatar is always round, so
  it reads as a person. Use an image with its own shape.
- **For status through colour.** The avatar's `color` means nothing. Use
  `status` for presence, and print the status where people act on it (an
  assignee picker): a dot is a glance, not the only cue.
- **For a long list of people.** A group shows who at a glance; past a
  handful, show the count or a list of names instead.
- **For an icon in a circle.** Use the icon itself, or an icon-only
  [Button](?path=/docs/components-buttons-button--docs) when it acts.

## Accessibility

- `imageAlt` names the **whole avatar** (`role="img"`), not just the
  image, so the image, the initials and the silhouette all announce the
  same person. The inner `<img>` has `alt=""`.
- Without `imageAlt` the avatar is **decorative** and hidden
  (`aria-hidden`), its status dot with it.
- With `status`, the status **joins the name**: "Mary Thompson, Online".
  The dot itself is not read.
- In a group, the "+n" is named "3 more" when the avatars are named, and
  decorative when they are not.
- With `hasTooltips`, the "+n" is the one avatar that takes focus, with
  the focus ring, and its tooltip of hidden names is its description. The
  other avatars' tooltips open on hover only: their names are already
  their accessible names.
- Initials are never read out: with `imageAlt` the avatar's name replaces
  them, without it the avatar is hidden. They are uppercased with CSS, so
  the text keeps the letters as passed.
- Initials meet 4.5:1 in every colour and variant, on every gray ramp in
  both modes, measured by the `Contrast` story. Neutral solid is gray 600
  under gray 50, 6.5:1 or more.
- The silhouette uses the initials colour (neutral subtle uses
  `--color-text-subtlest`), past the 3:1 a graphic needs in every colour
  and variant, measured by the `Contrast` story.
