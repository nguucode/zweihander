# Rating

<DocTabs>
<DocTab value="overview">

Asks for, or shows, a score out of a few stars: a review, a template's
rating, a difficulty.

## Variants

A rating either asks for a score or shows one.

| Mode | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| <Rating label="Interactive" defaultValue={3} /> | Asking: a review form, rating a template. Whole stars only. |
| <Rating readOnly value={4.5} aria-label="Read-only" /> | Showing: an average beside a product. Fractions draw as a star filled part-way. |

<Canvas of={RatingStories.ReadOnly} />

<Canvas of={RatingStories.Sizes} />

stars:

## Color

<SpecTable
  rows={[
    { part: 'Filled star', property: 'Colour', token: '--warning' },
    { part: 'Empty star', property: 'Colour', token: '--control-border' },
    { part: 'Label', property: 'Colour', token: '--foreground' },
    { part: 'Focus ring', property: 'Colour', token: '--ring' },
  ]}
/>

The fill is the bright `--warning` amber, not the darker warning text
colour: a rating reads as a rating when it looks like one. It is 2.1:1 on
white, below the 3:1 a meaningful graphic needs, so colour is not what
carries the score. A filled star differs from the outlined empty one by
shape, and the group or image is named "3 out of 5 stars".

- **Hover** previews the score up to the star under the pointer, and only
  while the rating can be changed.
- **Focus** is a 2px `--ring` outline round the focused star.
- **Disabled** dims the stars to 50%.
- **Read-only** is one image; fractions fill a star part-way, cut rather
  than shrunk.

## When to use

Use a rating to ask for, or show, a quick score on a short scale: a
review, a difficulty, how useful something was. Keep the scale short:
five stars is the convention readers already know.

<DoDont
  do={<Story of={RatingStories.DoFiveStars} />}
  doCaption="Five stars: few enough to hit and to tell apart at a glance."
  dont={<Story of={RatingStories.DontTenStars} />}
  dontCaption="Ten stars are small targets, and 7 looks much like 8. Use a Slider or a Number Input for a fine scale."
/>

## When not to use

- **For a precise number.** Use a
  [Slider](?path=/docs/components-controls-slider--docs) or a Number Input.
- **For a choice between named options.** Use a
  [Radio](?path=/docs/components-controls-radio--docs) group, so the
  options say what they mean.
- **To show progress.** Use a Progress Bar.

## Accessibility

- Interactive: a `<fieldset>` named by its legend (or `aria-label`), with
  one radio per star named "1 out of 5 stars" and so on (change the words
  with `getValueText`). Arrows move the choice; Tab moves into and out of
  the group.
- Read-only: `role="img"` named with the value, "4.5 out of 5 stars".
- Hover previews the score; focus shows the 2px ring round the star.
- A filled star differs from an empty one by shape as well as colour.
- Stars grow a 44px target on coarse pointers.
