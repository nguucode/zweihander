# Rating

Asks for, or shows, a score out of a few stars: a review, a template's
rating, a difficulty. Spec:
[uiguideline.com/components/rating](https://www.uiguideline.com/components/rating).
Built on native radio buttons; no Base UI part covers it.

## Anatomy

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Label** | Optional legend over the stars; names the group. |
| **Star** | Filled in `--warning-text` up to the value, outlined in `--control-border` after it. 24px (16px at `sm`, 32px at `lg`). |

### Design notes

- **Native radios, not a custom widget.** A rating is "pick one of five",
  which is what a radio group is: arrows, Tab, focus and form
  submission come from the browser, and a screen reader already knows
  how to read it. Each radio sits invisibly over its star.
- **Stars in the bright `--warning` amber.** A rating reads as a
  rating when it looks like one, so the fill is the amber solid, not the
  darker warning text colour. It is 2.1:1 on white, below the 3:1 a
  meaningful graphic needs, so colour is not what carries the score: a
  filled star differs from the outlined empty one (`--control-border`,
  3:1) by shape, and the group or image is named "3 out of 5 stars".
- **Read-only is one image.** "4.5 out of 5 stars" is one fact, so it is
  read once, and a fraction draws as a star filled part-way.

## Accessibility

- Interactive: a `<fieldset>` named by its legend, with one radio per
  star named "1 out of 5 stars" and so on (change the words with
  `getValueText`). Arrows move the choice; Tab moves into and out of
  the group.
- Hover previews the score up to the star under the pointer; focus shows
  the 2px ring around the star.
- Read-only: `role="img"` named with the value, "4.5 out of 5 stars".
- Stars are 16px at `sm` and grow a 44px target on coarse pointers.
