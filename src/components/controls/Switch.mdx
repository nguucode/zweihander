## Variants

`variant` is the colour of the on state. Off is the same in every variant.

| --- | --- |
| <Switch defaultChecked label="Primary" /> | The default: most settings. |

| <Switch variant="destructive" defaultChecked label="Destructive" /> | A setting that is risky to leave on: public sharing, deleting old data automatically. |

## Measurements

Two tracks, Atlassian's: 32×16px (`md`, the default) and 40×20px (`lg`).
Each is twice as wide as it is tall, so the thumb travels one track
height. The thumb sits 2px inside the track at both sizes.

`size="sm"` is deprecated. It draws the `md` track, warns once in
development, and will be removed in the next release: drop the prop.
`md` (the default, written out or not) and `lg` are silent.

## States

<States
  states={[
    { label: 'Off' },
    { label: 'On', content: <Switch defaultChecked label="Email notifications" /> },
    { label: 'Focus', pseudo: 'focus-visible' },
    { label: 'Disabled', content: <Switch disabled defaultChecked label="Email notifications" /> },
    { label: 'Error', content: <Switch validationState="error" label="Email notifications" /> },
  ]}
>
  <Story of={SwitchStories.Default} />
</States>

- **Hover** changes only the cursor: the track has no lighter or darker
  step to move to without losing its 3:1.
- **Focus** is a 2px `--color-border-focused` outline, 2px outside the track.
- **Disabled** dims the track and the label to 50%.
- **Error** draws a 2px `--color-text-danger` ring outside the track, as
  Figma's outside stroke. It fills the 2px focus offset, so the focus ring
  draws just beyond it and both show.
- **Read-only** looks enabled, with an arrow cursor.
- The thumb slides over `--duration-base`, and stops animating under
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Differences from the spec

- **Atlassian's two tracks.** 32×16px and 40×20px; `sm` is deprecated and
  draws the `md` track.
- **The label sits beside the track at regular weight**, as a Checkbox's
  does. Atlassian's toggle draws no visible label of its own (its `label`
  is hidden text for a screen reader); a form there puts a separate form
  Label next to it.
- **Base UI's names:** `checked`, `defaultChecked`, `onCheckedChange` for
  the spec's `defaultValue` and `onChange`; native `disabled`,
  `required`, `readOnly` for `isDisabled`, `isRequired`, `isReadOnly`.
- **`helperText` is added.** `validationState="error"` needs somewhere to
  say what is wrong; colour alone is not a message.

## When to use

Use a switch for a setting that takes effect the moment it is flipped,
like a light switch: dark mode, notifications, auto-save.

  dont={<Story of={SwitchStories.DontWaitForSave} />}
  dontCaption="A switch that waits for Save looks done when it is not. Use a checkbox in a form."
/>

## When not to use

- **For a choice submitted with a form.** Use a
  [Checkbox](?path=/docs/components-controls-checkbox--docs).
- **For more than two options.** Use a
  [Radio](?path=/docs/components-controls-radio--docs) group or a Select.
- **For a toggle in a toolbar** (Bold, a filter). Use a
  [Toggle Button](?path=/docs/components-buttons-togglebutton--docs).

## Accessibility

- `role="switch"` with `aria-checked`; Space toggles it.
- The label wraps the control, so it names it and clicking it toggles it.
  Without a visible label, pass `aria-label`: it goes to the switch itself.
- `helperText` is the switch's description (`aria-describedby`); in the
  error state the switch has `aria-invalid`.
- The off track is `--color-border-bold`, measured at 3:1 on the page on
  every gray ramp; the thumb is 4.5:1 or more against its track in both
  states.
- A hidden `<input type="checkbox">` carries `name`, `value` and
  `required` into native form submission and validation.
- On is the end side: in a right-to-left page the thumb travels left.
- The hit area is at least 24px tall, 44×44px on coarse pointers.
