# Grid List

Records as cards in a grid: people on a team, projects, files and photos.
Each card names the record, says a little about it, and either opens it
or offers its actions. After Tailwind UI Application UI, Lists: Grid
Lists.

```sh
npx shadcn@latest add https://ontheshore.biz/zweihander/r/grid-list.json
```

A card takes the same item as a [Stacked
List](?path=/docs/patterns-application-ui-stacked-list--docs) row, plus an
optional `cover`, so a list can switch between rows and cards without
reshaping its data. Use cards when the picture or the record's identity
matters more than comparing facts down a column; use Stacked List when
people scan and compare.

## Simple

An Avatar or an Icon, the `title` with a `description` under it, and
`meta` under that: an email, an owner, a date.

<Canvas of={GridListStories.Simple} />

## Linked

`href` makes the title a link whose target is the whole card. `status`
takes a Tag (or a Badge) and puts it after the title.

<Canvas of={GridListStories.Linked} />

## With actions

`actions` takes [Menu](?path=/docs/components-navigation-menu--docs)
entries, as in Stacked List and Table's `rowActions`: a ⋯ button in the
card's corner, named "Actions for" the title, and a [Context
Menu](?path=/docs/components-navigation-context-menu--docs) on the card
with the same entries. A card with `href` keeps the browser's own link
menu instead, so there the ⋯ button is the only way in.

<Canvas of={GridListStories.WithActions} />

A card can link and have actions: the menu sits above the card's link, so
a click on it opens the menu, not the record. Keep `status`,
`description` and `meta` free of controls on a linked card: the link
covers them.

<Canvas of={GridListStories.LinkedWithActions} />

## With a cover

`cover` puts a picture across the top of the card, edge to edge, at
16:9: a file preview, a photo, a logo. It is decorative, so give the
`<img>` `alt=""`; the title names the card.

<Canvas of={GridListStories.WithCover} />

## Footer and empty

The card panels, the footer and the empty state come from [List
Container](?path=/docs/patterns-application-ui-list-container--docs),
always in its `separate` appearance: a card is its own panel, so there is
no `flat` or `card` grid. `footer` goes after the grid; `empty` shows in
place of it, "Nothing here yet." by default.

Every `<div>` attribute passes through to the root, so a page names the
grid with `role="region"` and `aria-label` when it has no heading of its
own.

## Layout

- As many columns at least 16rem wide as the container fits, measured
  on the container rather than the viewport: three in 840px, one in a
  narrow aside or on a phone. There is no breakpoint of its own.
- A lone card stays one column wide instead of stretching across the
  row.
- Cards in a row are as tall as the tallest; text starts at the top.
- Long titles, emails and paths break anywhere rather than overflow.

## Differences from the spec

- **Column count** follows the container, not fixed counts per viewport
  breakpoint as in Tailwind, so the grid works in a side panel too.
- **Actions** are a ⋯ Menu, not a row of buttons along the bottom of the
  card (Tailwind's contact cards with Email and Call): two or more
  actions fit one menu at every width, and the card stays one shape.
- **Centred contact cards** (avatar above the name) are left out: one
  layout, media beside the title, reads the same as a Stacked List row.

## Accessibility

- The cards are an unordered list, so a screen reader announces how many
  there are. Reading and tab order go left to right, then down.
- The media and the cover are hidden from assistive tech: the title
  names the record.
- A card with `href` is one link, named by its title; the status,
  description and meta sit outside it.
- Each card is one tab stop, two with actions: the link, then the ⋯
  button.
- The ⋯ button is named "Actions for" the title; keep `title` a string
  when there are actions. The context menu is a shortcut to the same
  entries, never the only way to reach one.
- The grid has no landmark or heading of its own. Put it under a
  heading, or pass `role="region"` and an `aria-label`.
