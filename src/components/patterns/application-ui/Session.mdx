# Session

The two ends of a session: **Session Timeout**, the warning a minute
before an idle session ends, and **Signed Out**, the page the reader lands
on after it ends, with the account they were signed in to and **Sign in
again**. After Carbon's login pattern. Built on
[Modal](?path=/docs/overlays-modal--docs) (as an alert dialog), the **Auth
Layout** (see [Sign In](?path=/docs/templates-auth-sign-in--docs#auth-layout)),
Button and Link.

Neither part knows about sessions. The app tracks inactivity, opens the
warning, extends or ends the session from its buttons, and picks the
reason the signed-out page gives.

```sh
npx shadcn@latest add https://ontheshore.biz/zweihander/r/session.json
```

## Session timeout warning

Open it `countdown` seconds (60 by default) before an idle session ends.
The time left counts down in the sentence. **Stay signed in** has focus,
since that is what most readers want; it and Escape call `onContinue`,
where you extend the session and close the warning. **Sign out** calls
`onSignOut`. At zero, `onExpire` (or `onSignOut` when you leave it out):
sign out and show Signed Out with reason `inactive`.

<Canvas of={SessionStories.Warning} />

Keep `countdown` at 20 seconds or more: WCAG 2.2.1 (Timing Adjustable)
asks for a warning and at least 20 seconds to extend with a simple action.
The countdown runs to a fixed end, so a background tab, whose timers the
browser slows down, still ends on time. Closing and reopening it starts
the count again.

## Signed out

The page after a session ends, with a title and a line for the `reason`:

- `signed-out` (default): the reader pressed Sign out.
- `inactive`: the warning ran out.
- `expired`: the server ended the session, for example a token reached
  its lifetime.

<Canvas of={SessionStories.SignedOutPage} />

### Remembered account

Pass `account` (an email or a username, masked if you need to) to say which
account **Sign in again** is for, and carry it in `signInHref` so the reader
only types a password: [Progressive Sign In](?path=/docs/templates-auth-progressive-sign-in--docs)
takes it as `defaultEmail`, which is also where its **Remember my email**
comes back. `otherAccountHref` adds **Use another account**. Leave
`account` out after a deliberate sign out on a shared computer.

## Layout

- **Session Timeout** is a small (24rem) modal, never wider than the
  screen. The buttons sit at the end of the footer, Stay signed in last.
- **Signed Out** follows the Auth Layout: one 25rem column centred on the
  canvas, in a card by default. A long account breaks anywhere instead of
  pushing the page sideways at 320px.

## Accessibility

- The warning is an `alertdialog` named by its title and described by its
  sentence, so a screen reader reads the time left once, when it opens.
  The time is a `timer`, which is not a live region: it does not announce
  every second.
- Focus moves to **Stay signed in** when the warning opens, and back to
  where it was when it closes. Enter keeps the session; Escape does too.
- Signed Out has one `h1`, the reason, inside `main`. **Sign in again** is
  the first control in the page, before Use another account.
