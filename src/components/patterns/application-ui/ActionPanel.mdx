# Action Panel

A [Card](?path=/docs/components-data-display-card--docs) that offers one action:
a title saying what it does, a description of what happens when you take
it, and the control that does it. For actions that stand on their own on a
settings or detail page, such as exporting data, renewing a plan or
deleting a workspace. After Tailwind UI Application UI, Forms: Action
panels.

```sh
npx shadcn@latest add https://ontheshore.biz/zweihander/r/action-panel.json
```

## Simple

A button under the description. The default, and the right one when the
description runs to two lines or more.

<Canvas of={ActionPanelStories.Simple} />

## Button at the end

`actionPlacement="end"` puts the action beside the text, for a short
description. It drops under the text when the card gets narrow.

<Canvas of={ActionPanelStories.ButtonAtEnd} />

## With a switch

A setting that takes effect the moment it changes, so there is no Save
button. Give the Switch an `aria-label` that matches the title: the
panel's title is not its label.

<Canvas of={ActionPanelStories.WithSwitch} />

## With an input

A small form, one field and its button, for an action that needs one
value. The form is yours: the panel lays out what you pass.

<Canvas of={ActionPanelStories.WithInput} />

## Danger

`variant="danger"` edges the panel in the destructive colour, in every
appearance, flat surface included. Use it only for what cannot be undone,
with a destructive Button, and confirm in an alert
[Modal](?path=/docs/components-overlays-modal--docs) (`isAlert`) before acting. Put it
last on the page.

<Canvas of={ActionPanelStories.Danger} />

## Props

<ArgTypes of={ActionPanelStories} />

Every other Card prop and `<div>` attribute passes through, except
`onClick`, `render` and `orientation`: the panel is not itself a control,
and its placement prop sets the direction.

## Layout

- **Below** stacks the text and the action, in the Card's gap.
- **End** keeps the text at least 16rem wide, then wraps the action under
  it, keeping its order.
- The description stops at 65 characters a line; long titles and words
  break anywhere rather than overflow.

## Differences from the spec

- **With link**: no slot of its own. Pass a [Link](?path=/docs/components-navigation-link--docs)
  as the `action`.
- **Simple well, with well**: not a variant. The tinted inset is the
  Card's look; pick it with `appearance` so the panel matches the other
  blocks on the page.
- **Danger** is added: Tailwind leaves a danger zone to a red button.

## Accessibility

- The panel is a `group` named by its title, so a screen reader names
  it when focus enters the control. The description is linked with
  `aria-describedby`; screen readers differ on reading a group's
  description, so the control's own name must make sense alone. The title
  is heard twice in reading order, as the heading and the group's name.
- The title is an `h2` by default. Use `headingLevel={3}` or `4` under a
  section heading, so the outline has no gaps.
- The danger edge is not the only warning: the title and the destructive
  button say it too, so it does not rest on colour.
- The control keeps its own name: a Switch needs an `aria-label`, a
  button names its action ("Delete workspace", not "Delete"), a field
  has its label.
