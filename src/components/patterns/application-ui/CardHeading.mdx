# Card Heading

The top row of a card: what the card is, a line of context, and what you
can do with it. Put it first inside a [Card](?path=/docs/data-display-card--docs);
the card's padding and gap space it from the content. After Tailwind UI
Application UI, Headings: Card headings.

```sh
npx shadcn@latest add https://ontheshore.biz/zweihander/r/card-heading.json
```

## Simple

A title and, optionally, one line of description.

## With action

The card's main action at the end of the title row. One primary button at

Who or what the card is about, as an Avatar or an Icon before the title.

## With a rule

put a [Divider](?path=/docs/atomic-elements-divider--docs) after the heading,
inside the card.

## Layout

- **Actions wrap, the title does not squeeze.** The title and description
  keep the row down to 16rem, then the actions drop underneath them,
  keeping their order.
- **Long titles break anywhere** rather than overflow.

<Canvas of={CardHeadingStories.Narrow} />

## Accessibility

- The title is an `h2` by default: a card on a page sits under the
  page's `h1`. Use `headingLevel={3}` or `4` when the card sits under a
  section heading, so the outline has no gaps.
- The media is decorative and hidden from assistive tech: the title
  already says who or what the card is about.
- An icon-only action needs an `aria-label`, as on any Button. When a card
  repeats on a page, make action names unique, e.g. "Edit Chelsea Hagon".
