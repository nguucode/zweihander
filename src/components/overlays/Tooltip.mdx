# Tooltip

<DocTabs>
<DocTab value="overview">

A short label that appears when you hover over or focus a control: the
name of an icon-only button, a keyboard shortcut, a one-line hint.

<Status of={TooltipStories} />

<Canvas of={TooltipStories.LongContent} />

## Variants

There is one tooltip. It opens on hover after a short delay and on
keyboard focus at once. Wrap a row of tooltips in `TooltipGroup` so that
once one has opened, moving to the next opens it without the delay:

<Canvas of={TooltipStories.Group} />

Only one tooltip is open at a time: opening one closes any other. Hover or
focus the triggers above to see them.

The container is as wide as its text, at most 20rem and never wider than
the room beside the trigger; longer text wraps. It opens 6px from the
trigger, on the `--layer-tooltip` layer, above popovers and modals, so a
tooltip on a control inside one still shows. It fades in over
`--duration-fast`.

## States

<States
  states={[
    { label: 'Closed', content: <Story of={TooltipStories.Closed} /> },
    { label: 'Open', content: <Story of={TooltipStories.OpenPicture} /> },
  ]}

- **Closed** until the pointer rests on the trigger for `delay` (300ms), or
  the trigger takes keyboard focus, which opens it at once.
- **Open** until Escape, blur or the pointer leaves, after `closeDelay`.
  Opening fades it in and scales it up from 96%.
- The trigger keeps the states of its own component; the tooltip has none
  of its own.

## When to use

Use a Tooltip for a label or a hint: the name of an icon-only button, a
keyboard shortcut, one line on what a control does. 300ms before it opens
is quick enough to feel responsive and long enough that tooltips do not
flash as the pointer crosses a toolbar.

  dont={<Story of={TooltipStories.DontEssentialInfo} />}

/>

## When not to use

- **For anything a reader needs to finish a task.** Put it on the page: a
  tooltip cannot be reached by touch, and it disappears the moment you
  look away.
- **For links, buttons or other interactive content.** Hover and touch
  cannot reach the tooltip. Use a
  [Popover](?path=/docs/components-overlays-popover--docs).
- **On a disabled button.** It is not focusable, so its tooltip never opens
  by keyboard. Put the reason it is disabled in text next to it.

## Accessibility

- It opens on hover after `delay` and on keyboard focus at once, and
  closes on Escape, blur or pointer leave.

  <Canvas of={TooltipStories.Default} />

- Base UI keeps tooltips out of the accessibility tree. This component
  gives the popup `role="tooltip"` and, while it is open, points the
  trigger's `aria-describedby` at it, so it is read as a description.
- It is a **description, not a name.** An icon-only button still needs its
  own `aria-label`; the tooltip then shows that label to sighted users too.
- The trigger must be focusable: a Button, a link, a field.
- `--background` on `--foreground` reads at 16:1 or more in both modes.
