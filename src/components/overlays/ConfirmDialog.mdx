# Confirm Dialog

<DocTabs>
<DocTab value="overview">

Asks before something that cannot be undone: a warning, the action as a
question, what it will destroy, then Cancel or the action. A press outside
does nothing, so the reader has to choose.

<Status of={ConfirmDialogStories} />

<Canvas of={ConfirmDialogStories.Default} />

## Variants

| Variant | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| Default | An everyday delete: a comment, a file, a saved view. One question, two buttons. |
| Type to confirm (`confirmText`) | A large delete that cannot be undone: a workspace, an account, a project with its files. The action waits until the reader types the name. |

## States

The dialog is open or closed. With `confirmText`, the action is disabled
until the field matches, then enabled; without it, it is enabled from the
start.

### Differences from the spec

- **Destructive only.** There is no neutral variant: a decision that
  destroys nothing (sign out, leave without saving) is a
  [Modal](?path=/docs/components-overlays-modal--docs) with `isAlert`, where
  you choose the buttons.
- **No pending state.** The dialog closes on confirm. If the delete can fail,
  control `open` and keep it open until your request settles.

## When to use

Before an action that destroys something the reader cannot get back. Put
the object's name in the title, say in the description what goes with it,
and name the action button after what it does.

Type to confirm is for the few deletes that are both large and final. Used
on every delete, it becomes a chore readers do without reading.

<DoDont
  do={<Story of={ConfirmDialogStories.DoEverydayDelete} />}
  doCaption="Ask once for an everyday delete, with the action on the button."
  dont={<Story of={ConfirmDialogStories.DontTypeForSmall} />}
  dontCaption="Do not make the reader type for a small delete: it teaches them to type without reading."
/>

## When not to use

- **When the action can be undone.** Do it, then offer Undo in a
  [Toast](?path=/docs/components-notifications-toast--docs): no question
  is faster than a good one.
- **For a decision that destroys nothing**, such as Sign out or Leave
  without saving. Use a [Modal](?path=/docs/components-overlays-modal--docs)
  with `isAlert`.
- **To show a warning.** Use an
  [Inline Alert](?path=/docs/components-notifications-inlinealert--docs).

## Accessibility

- `role="alertdialog"`, named by the title (the icon is hidden from
  assistive tech) and described by the consequence.
- Focus starts on Cancel, so Enter on open cannot delete. With
  `confirmText` it starts in the field, which is named "To confirm, type"
  and the name.
- The action stays disabled until the field matches exactly, case
  included; spaces around the text are ignored. Enter in the field confirms
  once it matches.
- A press outside does nothing. Cancel and Escape close it without
  confirming, and focus goes back to the trigger.
- Reopened, the field starts empty.
