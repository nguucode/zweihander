# Popover

<DocTabs>
<DocTab value="overview">

A small panel anchored to a button, for content you interact with: a share
form, a filter, a set of options. It opens on click and stays until you
close it.

<Status of={PopoverStories} />

<Canvas of={PopoverStories.Open} />

## Variants

| Variant | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| Non-modal (default) | Most popovers. Tab can leave it and the page stays usable while it is open. |
| Modal (`isModal`) | A popover that must be finished or closed first: focus stays inside, the page does not scroll and presses outside are blocked. |
| `openOnHover` | A preview that also opens on hover, when what it holds must be reachable by click, keyboard and touch. For a person or record preview on a link, use [HoverCard](?path=/docs/components-overlays-tooltip--docs). |

A title is optional. Without one, name the popover with `aria-label`:

The container is as wide as its content, at most 22rem and never wider
than the room beside the trigger. It has no arrow: it opens 6px from the
trigger and lines up with it, which already ties the two. It has a 1px
border and a semibold title. The close button is 20px: 2px around its icon,
one line of text tall. Popovers sit on the `--layer-overlay` layer and fade
in over `--duration-fast`.

## States

The popover is open or closed. Opening fades it in and scales it up from
97%, from the trigger's side. The trigger keeps the states of its own
component (see Button); the close button is the only part with states of
its own:

## When to use

Use a Popover for a short task that belongs to the button that opens it:
share a file, set a filter, pick from a few options. It holds links,
inputs and buttons, which a Tooltip must not.

<DoDont
  do={<Story of={PopoverStories.DoShortTask} />}
  doCaption="A short task tied to its button: one field and one action."
  dont={<Story of={PopoverStories.DontLongForm} />}
  dontCaption="A whole form in a popover. It closes on a stray press outside and loses the work: use a Modal."
/>

## When not to use

- **For a label or a hint** on hover or focus. Use a
  [Tooltip](?path=/docs/components-overlays-tooltip--docs).
- **For a list of actions or commands.** Use a
  [Menu](?path=/docs/components-navigation-menu--docs): it has the
  arrow-key navigation a list of commands needs.
- **For a long form or a decision with consequences.** Use a
  [Modal](?path=/docs/components-overlays-modal--docs).

## Accessibility

- The popup is a `role="dialog"` named by `title` (or `aria-label`) and
  described by `description`. The trigger carries `aria-expanded` and
  `aria-haspopup`.
- Opening moves focus into the popup; Escape or a press outside closes it
  and returns focus to the trigger.
- **Non-modal by default:** Tab can leave the popup, and the page stays
  usable. `isModal` switches to Base UI's modal mode: focus is trapped,
  page scroll is locked and presses outside are blocked.
- Base UI only traps focus when the popup contains a close button, and a
  touch screen reader needs one to get out. So a modal popover without
  `hasCloseButton` still renders one: hidden until it has keyboard focus,
  then shown in the corner, so focus is never invisible.

  <Canvas of={PopoverStories.Modal} />

- `openOnHover` adds hover to click, it does not replace it: the content
  stays reachable by keyboard and touch.
- The close button has the 2px focus ring and a 44px hit area on touch
  screens.
