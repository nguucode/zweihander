# Spacing

Pick a spacing token by **what the gap does**, not by its size. Six roles
cover every gap and pad in the kit; a **density**, Default or Compact,
decides the number behind each one. A designer never chooses between 12
and 16 pixels: they choose `--space-cluster` or `--space-group`, and the
product's density does the rest.

## The six roles

| Token | Use for | Default | Compact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--space-stack` | Lines of text under each other: title to description, label to value | 4 | 2 |
| `--space-inline` | Small things side by side: icon to text, label to value on one line, sidebar items | 8 | 4 |
| `--space-cluster` | Controls or parts that sit together: filters in a toolbar, an avatar to its content, the parts of a page heading, inside a small card | 12 | 8 |
| `--space-group` | Items and blocks that belong together: cards in a row, toolbar to table, table cell sides | 16 | 12 |
| `--space-container` | Inside the edge of a card, panel or page heading | 24 | 16 |
| `--space-section` | Page edge and between page sections | 24 | 20 |

The scale is short on purpose: 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24. In Compact each
role takes the Default value of the role below it; only section steps
24 to 20. Section and container share 24px in Default, so separate
sections with a card edge or a rule, not with extra white space. AppShell
applies `--space-section` to the page edge from 64rem up; below that the
edge stays at its narrow value.

The numeric steps (`--space-1` to `--space-12`) still exist for the few
places no role fits, such as the size of an icon disc. They do not follow
density.

## Density

Density changes four things together, and keeps every size:

- **The six roles**, as in the table above.
- **The control tier.** Every tier exists in both densities. Compact
  gives each tier the padding and gap of the tier below it; md drops from
  32 to 28px, sm from 24 to 22px, xs stays at 20px (the floor of its text).
- **Type.** Body text is 14px in Default and 13px in Compact. Text of 12px
  and below never shrinks; 13 to 16px drops 1px; headings from 20px up
  drop one step. Compact body leading is 13/18, off the 4px grid on
  purpose. See [Typography](?path=/docs/foundations-typography--docs).
- **Component sizes**: the checkbox and radio box (`--choice-box`, 20 to
  16), the radio dot, the three switch tracks, the progress bar heights,
  avatars xs to lg (`--avatar-md`, 40 to 32), badge heights and dots, and
  table rows (`--row-height`, 48 to 36). In Compact each size takes the
  size below it; the smallest switch, avatar and badge step down further
  so sizes stay apart.

| Control token | md (D / C) | sm (D / C) | xs (D / C) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--control-{tier}` height | 32 / 28 | 24 / 22 | 20 / 20 |
| `--control-{tier}-pad` (fields, rows) | 10 / 8 | 8 / 6 | 6 / 4 |
| `--control-{tier}-pad-spacious` (buttons) | 12 / 8 | 8 / 6 | 6 / 4 |
| `--control-{tier}-gap` (icon to label) | 8 / 6 | 6 / 4 | 4 / 2 |

A small control inside a component (a table's sort button, a modal's
close button, a badge, the number input's steppers) reads the control tier
of its own size, like any other control, not a spacing role.

## Setting it

Density is set like appearance: on the root of the app, through `Theme`.

```tsx
<Theme density="compact">
  <App />
</Theme>
```

`Theme` writes `data-density` on its element, and every token picks its
number from the nearest one. A nested `Theme density="default"` inside a
compact app gets Default back. Popups portal to `<body>`, so set density
at the root; for a compact panel inside a default page, pass the panel
element as `container` to Popover, Menu, Tooltip, Modal, Select, Combobox
and DatePicker so their popups render inside it.

Density and `scaling` are independent. Scaling multiplies every size by
one factor (12px becomes 11.2px at 93%); density picks each number,
keeps whole pixels and holds the 12px floor. Both can be set at once.

In Storybook, the **Density** toolbar switches every story between the two.

## Touch and rem

Controls are below the 44px touch minimum in both densities on purpose:
on coarse pointers every control widens its hit area to 44px without
growing the box, so Compact does not cost touch users. Every value is in
`rem`, so it follows the user's root font size; "32px" is 32px at the
default 16px root.
