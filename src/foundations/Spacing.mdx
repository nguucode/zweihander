# Spacing

Pick a spacing token by **what the gap does**, not by its size. Six roles
cover every gap and pad in the kit, and each role has one value. A
designer never chooses between 12 and 16 pixels: they choose
`--space-cluster` or `--space-group`.

<Canvas of={SpacingStories.Roles} />

## The six roles

| Token | Use for | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--space-stack` | Lines of text under each other: title to description, label to value | 4 |
| `--space-inline` | Small things side by side: icon to text, label to value on one line, sidebar items | 8 |
| `--space-cluster` | Controls or parts that sit together: filters in a toolbar, an avatar to its content, the parts of a page heading, inside a small card | 12 |
| `--space-group` | Items and blocks that belong together: cards in a row, toolbar to table, table cell sides | 16 |
| `--space-container` | Inside the edge of a card, panel or page heading | 24 |
| `--space-section` | Page edge and between page sections | 24 |

The scale is short on purpose: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24. Section and container
share 24px, so separate sections with a card edge or a rule, not with
extra white space. Apply `--space-section` to the page edge from 64rem
up; below that, keep the edge at its narrow value.

The numeric steps (`--space-0-5` to `--space-20`) still exist for the few
places no role fits, such as the size of an icon disc.

## Controls

The control tier binds a control's height, padding and icon gap, so a
control never reads a spacing role for its own box.

| Control token | lg | md | sm | xs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--control-{tier}` height | 40 | 32 | 24 | 20 |
| `--control-{tier}-pad` (rows, menu items) | – | 10 | 8 | 6 |
| `--control-{tier}-pad-spacious` (buttons, from the outer edge to the label) | – | 12 | 12 | 6 |
| `--control-{tier}-gap` (icon to label) | – | 6 | 6 | 4 |

A field is `lg` tall by default and `md` at `size="sm"`, one step above a
button. It takes the numeric step `--space-1-5` (6px) inside its edge at
both sizes, and `--space-stack` between its label, box and helper text.

A small control inside a component (a table's sort button, a modal's
close button, a badge, the number input's steppers) reads the control tier
of its own size, like any other control, not a spacing role.

## One scale

The kit has one set of numbers: there is no density mode and no scaling
factor. Where a screen needs a tighter control, such as a button in a
table row or a filter in a toolbar, use that component's `size="sm"`.

## Touch and rem

Controls are below the 44px touch minimum on purpose: on coarse pointers
every control widens its hit area to 44px without growing the box. Every
value is in `rem`, so it follows the user's root font size; "32px" is 32px
at the default 16px root.
